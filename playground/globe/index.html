        THREEBASE / GLOBE

        A closer look at Earth.

        Explore countries and territories by touching the globe or searching. Tiny places are easiest to find with search.

        A map, with context

        Boundaries follow Natural Earth’s generalized map units. They are for exploration, not navigation or legal use. Disputed borders and statuses are not resolved by this map; inclusion does not imply recognition.

        Data & imagery

        • Natural Earth — public-domain boundaries and supplementary population estimates.
        • World Bank — population estimates. Each card shows the available year; figures are not live.
        • Countries by mledoze — names, capitals, languages and currencies (ODbL).
        • NASA Blue Marble — Earth imagery, distributed with Three Globe. Cloud texture via WebGL Earth.

        Data snapshot: . Lighting and clouds are illustrative, not live weather. Some small islands have a location marker where this map has no outline.
